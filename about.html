Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Founded in 2023, EcoCycle Solutions was established to encourage sustainable environmental practices throughout South Africa. Our goal is to reduce pollution, promote recycling, and educate communities about environmental responsibility.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide accessible recycling services and environmental education that encourage sustainable living for all South Africans — regardless of location or income.

🌍 Our Vision

To create cleaner communities and inspire individuals to actively protect the environment, contributing to a sustainable future for South Africa.

💚 Our Values

Accountability, community empowerment, environmental stewardship, inclusivity, and innovation drive everything we do at EcoCycle Solutions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about EcoCycle Solutions and our services.

You can schedule a collection by completing our online enquiry form, calling us on 068 285 2438, or sending a WhatsApp message. We typically confirm collections within 2–3 business days.

Absolutely! We love working with educational institutions. We offer specially designed workshop programmes, in-class presentations, and on-campus recycling initiatives. Send an enquiry to discuss a custom programme for your school.

We welcome volunteers for community clean-ups, educational events, and administrative support. Complete our volunteer enquiry form and our outreach team will contact you within 3 business days.

We accept paper, cardboard, glass, metals (aluminium and tin), and most plastics (PET, HDPE, PVC). We do not currently collect electronic waste or hazardous materials. Download our Recycling at Home guide for a full list.
